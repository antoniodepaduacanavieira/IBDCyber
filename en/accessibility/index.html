Accessibility

The portal is designed for different screen sizes and keyboard navigation, with particular attention to content structure.

Navigation features

Descriptive links, heading structure, visible focus and a skip link guide the implementation. Browser zoom can enlarge the text. The language switch opens the equivalent content where available.

Reference and limits

WCAG 2.2 Level AA is the design reference. The tests recorded in the dossier describe what was checked. This page does not claim certification or complete conformance. External documents and websites may provide different accessibility conditions.

Reporting a difficulty

If you encounter a barrier, identify the page, what you tried to do and, where possible, your browser and assistive technology. Institutional channels are listed on the contact page.
