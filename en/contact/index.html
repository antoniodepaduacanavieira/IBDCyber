Available channels

Consult @institutociberbr on Instagram and the current institutional website. These are the public channels identified for this version. This prototype does not receive messages or provide a contact form.

Introducing a subject

  • Institutional discussion: describe the subject and the people or organisations involved.
  • Research and education: explain the question or need and the intended audience.
  • Cooperation and media: state the purpose, context and any relevant deadline.

Before sending

Avoid passwords, personal documents, information about third parties or confidential material in an initial message. This institutional channel is not presented as an incident response service.
