From purpose to activity

The areas presented here reflect the purpose described by the Institute. The activity archive contains reported or documented events. Any new engagement requires agreement on its objectives, participants, scope and conditions.

Four areas of work

  • Public policy: institutional and legislative choices in cybersecurity.
  • Research: questions, evidence and references for Brazil's policy debate.
  • Education: understanding digital risk and preparing for decisions.
  • Cooperation: discussion across sectors and exchange of experience.

Introduce a request

Describe the subject, the people involved and the decision or difficulty behind your request. This gives the team a basis for considering a conversation. This prototype does not accept orders, course registrations or incident reports.
