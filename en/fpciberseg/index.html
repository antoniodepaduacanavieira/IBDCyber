The relationship with IBDCyber

The group was installed on 25 March 2025. Senate records name Carlos Diego de Araújo Pinto e Lima as Executive Secretary. IBDCyber identifies him as its President. These are distinct roles and do not establish legal subordination between the institutions.

Follow the bill

Checked on 17 October 2026, Bill 4752/2025 remained under consideration, assigned to Senator Jorge Seif at CCT. Its latest recorded status was dated 3 September 2026. It is a legislative proposal, not enacted law.
